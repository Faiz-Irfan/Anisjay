01 / THE STORY

THE NAME
IS JAY.

I'm Nur Anis Mohd Faruk, a Johor girl and the youngest of four. My abah gave me the nickname "Jay" after bodybuilding legend Jay Cutler. His journey is what got me into the gym in the first place.

My family didn't support it at first, and plenty of people still think lifting makes a woman "less pretty" or weak. I kept training anyway, worked on my technique, and they came around once they saw what it did for me. Now I coach men and women to do the same.
